At Co2Offset.ai I designed and built the cloud infrastructure behind a forest carbon-capture SaaS processing satellite and ground data: ECS / Fargate and EC2 for compute, RDS and S3 for state, Lambda for event-driven work, and IAM policies scoped per service. Every environment was provisioned with Terraform and configured with Ansible, so staging and production stayed identical by construction rather than by convention. That infrastructure carried the pipeline through the Bureau Veritas audit under SOC 2 and ISO 27001, which unlocked €2.5M of business.

## What I deliver

- **Terraform across environments** — modules, remote state and plan review, so an environment is reproducible instead of hand-built.
- **AWS designed for least privilege** — IAM roles and policies scoped per service, secrets kept out of images and repositories.
- **Ansible configuration management** — the gap between a provisioned machine and a working one, closed and version-controlled.
- **Hetzner Linux VPS fleets** — the same discipline applied where a managed cloud isn't the right cost: Docker, systemd and Terraform-managed infrastructure.
- **Cost and blast-radius review** — right-sized instances, separated accounts and environments, and a documented path back when a change goes wrong.
