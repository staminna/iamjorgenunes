Containers are where most of my production work lives. At Co2Offset.ai that meant Kubernetes running 400+ containerised microservice endpoints with Helm charts, auto-scaling and health probes in front of PostgreSQL / PostGIS, MongoDB, Redis and Kafka. On my own platforms it means Docker Compose with real parity between a developer's laptop and the production host, so what passes locally is what ships.

## What I deliver

- **Helm and Kustomize** — charts and overlays that make an environment a configuration choice, not a fork.
- **Gateway API with Gloo / Envoy** — routing, TLS termination and traffic policy at the edge of the cluster.
- **Health probes and autoscaling** — liveness and readiness that actually gate traffic, with scaling tied to the signal that matters.
- **Graceful shutdown** — SIGTERM draining so a deploy or a scale-down doesn't drop in-flight requests.
- **Local/production parity** — Docker Compose stacks that mirror production, plus test-gated `linux/amd64` image builds.
