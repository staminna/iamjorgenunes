AR experiences driven by the full modern smartphone sensor suite, built on the same real-time pipelines I use for installations and live performance: TouchDesigner, GLSL, OSC sensor I/O, iPhone LiDAR capture and 3D Gaussian Splatting.

## What I deliver

- Full smartphone sensor fusion: GPS/GNSS, gyroscope, accelerometer, magnetometer (compass), barometer, LiDAR/ToF depth, camera, ambient-light and proximity sensors as live inputs to the experience.
- Geo-anchored and world-tracked AR with ARKit / ARCore, and WebXR with device-orientation + geolocation for the mobile web.
- iPhone LiDAR depth capture → 3D Gaussian Splatting (COLMAP + splatfacto) for scan-to-scene reconstruction and projection-surface mapping.
- Local SLMs on-device — quantized Llama / Qwen / Mistral small models via Ollama — for offline, low-latency, private AI inside the experience.
- Real-time sensor I/O beyond the phone: DualSense 6-axis IMU, touchpad and haptics over HID→OSC into TouchDesigner.
