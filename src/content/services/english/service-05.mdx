Most of what keeps a platform alive happens on the host. At the European Central Bank that meant Red Hat Linux with Bash and Python automation, PowerShell across Microsoft Active Directory, a staging → preview → internal → live sync tool, a scheduled job queue, and log management built for auditable tracking of every operation — plus diagnosing escalated incidents and training 1st- and 2nd-level support. Today it means running my own Hetzner Linux hosts the same way.

## What I deliver

- **systemd services** — units with restart policies, health probes and graceful shutdown, not a process in a terminal.
- **Nginx and Apache** — reverse proxies, multi-vhost setups and TLS, configured and reviewed rather than copy-pasted.
- **Automation in Bash, Python and PowerShell** — the repetitive operational work moved into scripts under version control.
- **Logging and observability** — logs you can search when it matters, and an auditable record of operations.
- **Incident response** — escalated-incident diagnosis, production migrations and the documentation that lets someone else handle the next one.
